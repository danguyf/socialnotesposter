# Privacy Policy for Social Notes Poster

**Effective Date:** February 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy outlines how data is handled within the application.

---

## 1. Data Ownership and Storage
The App is entirely local. All notes, drafts, and configuration settings are stored locally on your device.

## 2. WordPress Integration
The app functions as a bridge between your device and your personal **WordPress site**. 
* When you post a note, the data is sent directly from your device to your WordPress site via the WordPress REST API.
* **Credentials:** Your WordPress login credentials (Application Passwords or tokens) are stored encrypted on your device and are never transmitted to the developer.

## 3. No "Phone Home" Policy
The app does not phone home.
* **No Analytics:** No tracking pixels, Google Analytics, or any other telemetry to monitor your behavior.
* **No Central Servers:** The app does not communicate with any server owned or operated by the developer. 
* **No Data Collection:** The developer does not collect, see, or have access to your IP address, device ID, or the content of your notes.

## 4. Third-Party Services

Since the app connects to your own WordPress instance, your data is subject to:
1. The Privacy Policy of your **WordPress Hosting Provider**.
2. The privacy settings and plugins you have configured on your **WordPress site**.

> **Note:** We recommend using HTTPS (SSL) on your WordPress site to ensure that the connection between the app and your site is encrypted and secure.

## 5. Permissions
The App may request the following permissions:
* **Internet/Network Access:** Required solely to communicate with your WordPress site.
* **Storage Access:** Required to save your notes and settings locally on your device.

## 6. Data Retention and Deletion
Because the developer does not store your data, the developer cannot delete it for you. 
* To delete your local data, you can simply uninstall the app or clear the app cache in your device settings.
* To delete posted notes, you must log in to your WordPress dashboard and delete them manually.

## 7. Changes to This Policy
Imight update this policy from time to time to reflect changes in the app's features. The "Effective Date" at the top will indicate when the latest changes were made.
